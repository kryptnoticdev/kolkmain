1. Introduction

King of Kols ($KOL) is a KOL-to-Earn protocol on Solana. It rewards holders and raiders for driving genuine social attention on X/Twitter. Unlike typical meme coins, Raid-to-Earn is live from Day 1.

Powered by a Telegram bot, $KOL automates wallet linking, X verification, raid tracking, leaderboards, Hall of Fame, and daily on-chain SOL distributions — creating a transparent, self-sustaining ecosystem where community engagement directly fuels token value through fees and burns.

Eligibility

To receive SOL rewards, all three conditions must be met:

  • Hold ≥ 1,000,000 $KOL in a linked Solana wallet — checked on-chain at every distribution
  • Link a Solana wallet via /linkwallet in the Telegram bot
  • Earn ≥ 1 raid point in the current 24h cycle
No Twitter Blue / verified badge required. Any holder who raids qualifies.

Dice Multiplier System

Before each raid, participants can roll their dice for a point multiplier:

  • Up to 3 rolls per raid — choose to keep a roll or try your luck again
  • Once you reroll, the previous result is gone — no going back
  • The 3rd roll is always final and locks automatically
  • Multiplier resets after each raid ends and after each 24h distribution

Dice tiers: 🎲 (1x) · 🎲🎲 (1.5x) · 🎲🎲🎲 (2x) · ⚡🎲⚡ (3x) · 🔥🎲🔥 (4x) · 👑🎲👑 (5x)

3.4 Reward Formula

The reward pool splits proportionally every 24 hours using a combined weight formula. No tiers, no caps on distribution, no complex normalization — 100% of the pool always distributes.

Formula
weight      =  min(token_balance, 30,000,000) × window_points
your_payout  =  ( your_weight / total_weight ) × pool
Token balance is capped at 30M — holding more gives no extra weight, so active raiders always matter. Points include any dice multiplier applied during the raid.

Example — 3 Participants

1 SOL pool
Person A:  30,000,000 tokens × 500 pts = 15,000,000,000 weight
Person B:   5,000,000 tokens × 200 pts =  1,000,000,000 weight
Person C:   1,000,000 tokens × 100 pts =    100,000,000 weight
Total weight: 16,100,000,000
Person A: 15B / 16.1B × 1 SOL = 0.9317 SOL
Person B:  1B / 16.1B × 1 SOL = 0.0621 SOL
Person C: 0.1B / 16.1B × 1 SOL = 0.0062 SOL
Total: 1.000 SOL distributed — 100% ✓
Unlike fixed-percentage systems, this formula works at any community size — 2 people or 2,000. The pool always fully distributes.
